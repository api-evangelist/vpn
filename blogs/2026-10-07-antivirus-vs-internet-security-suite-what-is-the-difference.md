The main difference between an antivirus and an internet security suite is how much protection they provide. Antivirus software is built to stop malware, while an internet security program goes further with extras like firewalls, phishing protection, and safer browsing tools. Many internet security programs also add parental controls, spam filters, or password managers to cover more of your online life.
