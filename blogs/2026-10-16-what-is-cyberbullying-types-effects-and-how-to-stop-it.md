Cyberbullying is repeated online abuse meant to embarrass, scare, or humiliate someone. It can happen on phones, computers, and tablets, through gaming platforms, social media, or messaging apps. This guide explains the main types of cyberbullying, how it affects people, when it may be illegal, how to prevent it, and where to get help.
